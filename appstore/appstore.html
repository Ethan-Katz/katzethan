My Apps

  • Button Bash!

    Button Bash! is a fast-paced arcade game that challenges your reflexes and speed. In this game, players are tasked with tapping buttons on the screen, earning points for speed and accuracy. Button Bash offers endless hours of fun, whether you're looking to kill some time or aiming to top the leaderboards, Button Bash is the perfect game for you. Test your reflexes, improve your speed.

  • Holy Stride

    Discover a unique way to intertwine fitness and faith as you traverse the beautiful landscapes of Israel. The app translates your steps into mitzvot according to halacha, honoring the tradition where every 4 amot walked equals one cherished mitzvah. Track your steps, embrace the spiritual essence of each stride, and embark on a journey that harmonizes physical activity with religious significance.

  • CouchPotatoLog

    Track every TV show and movie you watch, beautifully. CouchPotatoLog remembers exactly where you are in everything you are watching: which episode, which season, and how many are left. Up Next tells you what to watch tonight, Upcoming shows every air date for the shows you follow, and your library syncs across iPhone, iPad and the web. Free, with no ads and no tracking.
